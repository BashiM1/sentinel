Battle scar 08 — `go get` fails with DNS timeout in this sandbox
Captured: 2026-05-22, during Prompt 6 (audit subsystem)

=== The bite ===
$ go get github.com/google/uuid
go: module github.com/google/uuid: Get "https://proxy.golang.org/github.com/google/uuid/@v/list": dial tcp: lookup proxy.golang.org on 127.0.0.53:53: read udp 127.0.0.1:35294->127.0.0.53:53: i/o timeout

=== Discovery ===
The sandbox has no outbound DNS to proxy.golang.org. But the dep was already
in the module cache:

$ grep -E "uuid|google/uuid" go.sum
github.com/google/uuid v1.6.0 h1:NIvaJDMOsjHA8n1jAhLSgzrAzy1Hgr+hNrb57e+94F0=
github.com/google/uuid v1.6.0/go.mod h1:TIyPZe4MgqvfeYDBFedMoGGpEw/LqOeaOT+nhxU+yHo=

So google/uuid v1.6.0 was already an indirect dep (likely pulled in by
controller-runtime or kubernetes/api). Importing it directly and running
`go mod tidy` promoted it to a direct require without touching the network.

=== Why no terminal output for the resolution ===
`go mod tidy` exited silently — it just edited go.mod in place. The before/
after grep:

  Before:  github.com/google/uuid v1.6.0 // indirect
  After:   github.com/google/uuid v1.6.0
